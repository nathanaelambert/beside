# Playground

## Register

product

## Users

Beside's collaborators exploring and discussing the Subject, Activity, Log, and Participation model together in a browser. They need to switch perspectives and understand the effects of a single action without configuring infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

Make tentative model relationships testable. Create subjects and activities, submit logs through a common command boundary, and inspect ownership, progress, sharing, and event delivery. Persist a seeded relational dataset in the browser. Keep diagrams and SQL assumptions available beside the experiment.

## Brand Personality

Curious, approachable, clear. Use Beside's shared-activity context and warm, readable surfaces from the concept brief and storyboard, with greater density appropriate to a workbench.

## Anti-references

No game world, avatars moving around a map, or ornamental reward system. The user requested conventional cards, tables, and forms. Avoid opaque automation and progress numbers without an explanation.

## Design Principles

- Explain each mutation through its records, owner, and recipients.
- Distinguish private logging, sharing, and progress credit.
- Label draft assumptions and simulated integrations plainly.
- Keep the experiment easy to restart, export, and repeat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, labels and live feedback. Pair color with text and icons. Respect reduced motion. Support narrow screens and horizontally scrollable diagrams and tables.
