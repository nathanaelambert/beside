These principles govern every design choice in Beside.
The more a feature aligns with all principles, the better it is.

## 1. Useful
Beside helps users do meaningful things.
Beside provides context, progress tracking, communication, and motivation.
Beside is not be a digital distraction.
### 1.1 Good and Bad is clear beforehand
Before acting, users know if their action will have a good or bad outcome

## 2. Shared
Beside helps users see distant, asynchronous actions as belonging to one shared experience.
### 2.1 Action outcome is collectively decided
Users agree beforehand on what action is good or bad.
### 2.2 Sucess is achieved through collective cooperation only
Success is reached if all involved users do enough good actions.

## 3. Playful
Beside provides a peaceful, enjoyable, safe experience. 
### 3.1 Motivations are internal
Rewards are visual reminders of collective achievements. They provide no real-world benefit.

## 4. Impactful 
Every decision and every action has consequences. 
### 4.1 There is no generic currency to reward actions
In game currency blurs actions with their consequences.
### 4.2 Planning is a destructive action
Users commiting to a plan of action must sacrifice something they have.
